What it is

Parking Master Multiplayer 2 is an open-world driving and parking game where players share a city: meeting up, trading cars, showing off their builds and racing each other. It has passed 15 million downloads across iOS and Android and is still in live operation.

What I built

  • Shipped a broad range of player-facing systems into a live title at that scale.
  • Built the character customization system, multiplayer events, new game modes and a vehicle accessory system.
  • Optimized the vehicle customization system, and ran continuous memory and CPU work to hold performance across low-end Android and iOS hardware.

Systems I shipped

I built the character customization system, multiplayer events, new game modes and a vehicle accessory system, among others. The accessory system paid off straight away: the police siren it introduced became the month’s best-selling in-app purchase.

Performance on the hardware people actually own

I optimized the vehicle customization system and ran continuous memory and CPU work to hold smooth performance across low-end Android and iOS devices.

How it landed

The game has passed 15 million downloads across iOS and Android and remains in live operation. The vehicle accessory system I built produced the month’s best-selling in-app purchase, a police siren.
